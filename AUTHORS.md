# Authors and Contributions

## Angela

Angela worked on the project documentation and timing diagram of the project. 
Her contributions included writing the README documentation, explaining the overall CPU design, 
describing the pipeline stages, documenting the ISA design, writing demo instructions, making the overall diagram of the CPU, and creating the R-type, I-type, and J-type timing diagrams.

Derek worked on the main CPU implementation and the technical design tasks. 
His contributions included the SystemVerilog design files, datapath implementation, controller/decoder logic,
